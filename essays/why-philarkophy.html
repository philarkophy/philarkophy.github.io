Why Philarkophy

Ark Saini · August 2026

Every philosophy begins somewhere personal, whether it admits it or not. This one admits it in its name.

Philarkophy is the place where I intend to think in public: slowly, carefully, and without pretending to more certainty than I have. Some essays will wrestle with old questions — what a good life asks of us, why suffering needs meaning, what we owe each other. Others will start from something small: a sentence in a book, a conversation, a walk.

The unexamined life is not worth living — but the examined one still has to be lived.

That tension is roughly where this site lives. Thinking is not a substitute for living, and living without thinking tends to go badly. Essays here are my attempt to hold both at once.
