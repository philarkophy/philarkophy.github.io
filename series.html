Series

Six columns, one for each day of the working week, named for the sun on the emblem above. Sunday is for rest.

First Light

Monday · Philosophy

One idea from philosophy, looked at before the day gets busy. Ancient and modern, made my own, and tested against ordinary life.

Equinox

Tuesday · Abstract games

Two players, a board, an equal start. Rules, positions and the design of games in which there is nothing to blame but yourself.

Zenith

Wednesday · Work

The sun at its highest and the working hours. What I am building, the problems it exists to solve, and what the work teaches that I use everywhere else.

Solstice

Thursday · Archaeoastronomy

Stones, horizons and the sky the builders watched. How people without telescopes caught the turning of the year, and what they thought it meant.

Heliotrope

Friday · Living well

Named for the plant that turns to follow the sun. A phone with no apps, food counted carefully, training, rest: the rules I have chosen to live by, and which of them are really physics.

Afterglow

Saturday · The loose one

What is left when the week has gone down. Unplanned, unfiled, and the only column with no rules.

Every column, in one newsletter.
