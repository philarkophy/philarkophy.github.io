About

I'm Ark. I'm 25, and I've promised myself I'll write every day. Even one line. Even a phrase. It counts.

I grew up in a house loud with books and quiet with people — my mother is a professor of English literature, and I don't remember a time before books; there wasn't one. As a kid, when a conversation was too hard to have, I didn't have it. I wrote a letter instead. Slid under a door for my parents, tucked inside a book for my friends. Paper was where I was fluent. It still is.

Philarkophy is what happened when philosophy collided with my own name — and with my refusal to stay in one lane. This space holds philosophy, mostly, self-taught for now. And the rest of the puzzle I'm trying to put together: living without a phone in a glowing world, looking for a career that fits, moving through the world neurodivergent, building a self worth the trouble.

The fuller introduction is in my first letter, The Symphony — about the urge to learn everything, and why the disciplines are one piece of music.

One line a day. Some of them will land here, slid under your door. If you'd like them delivered, subscribe to my newsletter, or find me on Instagram.
